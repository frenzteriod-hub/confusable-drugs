Cebuano-aware · experimental

Search how a medicine may be read—even when it is misread

This tool compares official spelling with exported batch pronunciation paths for registered names. Each shown path carries a route, evidence level, source, IPA, coverage, and cost. Free text uses bundled contextual English G2P plus separately costed Cebuano-aware hypotheses; it can suggest a registered name but does not replace the batch result.

Important limitation: The rules are pronunciation hypotheses informed by current project research. They have not been validated as a complete model of Cebuano speakers and should only generate candidates for human review.
