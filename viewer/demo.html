    What the complete study does

    From FDA-derived names to expert reference labels

    1Generate candidates

    The research pipeline retrieves potentially confusable registered names while keeping written and spoken evidence separate.

    2Review independently

    All three experts receive the same score-free sample and choose LASA, Not LASA, or Needs discussion.

    3Evaluate afterward

    Only after independent review are expert labels compared with the hidden algorithm ranking for research evaluation.

    Candidate does not mean confirmed LASA. A pair remains a screening candidate until documented expert adjudication supplies the reference label.

    What is deliberately not included

    • Real expert-validation candidates, progress, comments, and identities.
    • Algorithm scores, formulas, weights, ranks, thresholds, strata, and recommendations.
    • The complete Philippine FDA-derived product directory; the reviewer preview shows only fictional evidence for the current pair.
    • Database writes, authentication, exports, and administrator-only research records.
